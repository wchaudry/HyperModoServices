HyperModo is my practice. I build software platforms and the teams that run them, and I have done it for twenty years, always close to data — databases, pipelines, integrations.

I wrote the strategy for a $3B company's digital platform, sold it to their executives, won a $4M programme, then hired and led the seventy people who built it. Before that I co-founded a company, raised $2.5M, and closed a $15M contract with the US Air Force research lab. Earlier: distributed SQL at NuoDB, data integration at Expressor (acquired by Qlik), bank fraud detection at Memento (acquired by FIS), marketing databases for Pfizer, Capital One and Bank of America at Epsilon, and four years at Rackspace running data-services strategy.

I am still in the code every day.

## What I offer

**Transformation strategy through delivery.** The business case, the platform, the team, the go-live. One owner from the first slide to production. This is [the programme](/work/enterprise-data-platform/).

**Fractional platform or engineering leadership.** Head of platform or CTO for a company that has the need and not the headcount.

**Engineering with AI agents.** {#engineering-with-ai-agents} For the past year most of the code on a live production system has been written by AI agents, with me directing and reviewing. That works because of the framework around it, not the prompting: persistent shared memory so nothing is re-investigated, a written definition of what a defect is, a review loop that runs until a round raises nothing, and rules that turn every correction into something the next session inherits. I set this up for teams.
