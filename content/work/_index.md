## Companies I helped build

Three roles, promoted twice. Distributed SQL database. Ran the launch, built the developer community, wrote the site, papers and demos, and got the product onto the Gartner Magic Quadrant for Operational DBMS.

### Expressor Software — Head of Product Marketing & Management, 2010–2012
High-speed ETL. Repositioned the product for business analysts and took it to 30,000 downloads a month; wrote the due-diligence material behind the acquisition by Qlik.

### Memento — Senior Product Manager, 2008–2010
Bank fraud detection. Expanded the product from internal fraud to check fraud, card bust-out, anti-money-laundering and regulatory reporting. Acquired by FIS.

### Epsilon — Senior Solutions Architect, 2005–2008
Marketing databases for Pfizer, Capital One and Bank of America. Designed the Bank of America credit-card marketing system that won a three-year, $83M contract — the largest in the company's history.
