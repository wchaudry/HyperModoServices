## The situation

Deploying and managing applications across geographically distributed infrastructure — before Kubernetes had an answer for it.

## What I did

Co-founded Nalej as Chief Product and Operations Officer. Raised the capital, ran a team of eighteen across product, engineering and field work in three countries, delivered a working version for Airbus in Madrid in ninety days, and led the CMMC and ATO compliance work that defence contracting requires.

## What happened

A $15M, five-year contract with DARPA and AFRL. $3M in professional services validating the product with Toshiba Memory America. The company is still operating, now focused on US government work.
