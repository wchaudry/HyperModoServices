## The situation

The program needed skills the client did not have — data modelling, pipeline engineering, platform work — and could not hire locally at the budget available.

## What I did

Designed the career program: a 12-month fresh-graduate program that starts after graduation and rotates new hires through DevOps, backend, frontend, product and security on real deliverables; a management track with executive mentorship; and an expert track to architect, principal and fellow. Ran campus recruiting personally at three universities and wrote the coding assessments myself.

## What happened

An organization that grew from nothing to seventy people, most of them through the program. More than fifty graduates stayed on permanently. The team shipped the platform described in [the program](/work/enterprise-data-platform/).
