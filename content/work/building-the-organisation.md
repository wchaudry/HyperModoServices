## The situation

The programme needed skills the client did not have — data modelling, pipeline engineering, platform work — and could not hire locally at the budget available.

## What I did

Designed a four-track career programme: an eight-week internship for final-year students; a nine-month graduate rotation through DevOps, backend, frontend, product and security; a management track with executive mentorship; and an expert track to architect, principal and fellow. Ran campus recruiting personally at three universities and wrote the coding assessments myself.

## What happened

An organisation that grew from nothing to seventy people, most of them through the programme. More than fifty graduates stayed on permanently. The team shipped the platform described in [the programme](/work/enterprise-data-platform/).
